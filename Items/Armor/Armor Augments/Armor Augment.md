Armor augments give each armor its own effect that allows you to stack utility.
The 3 rarities of augments are Atom, Fusion, and Singularity

Information:
- Rarity (overrides base)
- Effect
- Energy Type


Singularity Augments:
- [[Shared Orbit]]
- [[Elastic Cascade]]
- [[Thermal Shock]]
- [[Resonant Chorus]]
- [[Echo Chamber]]
- [[Snapback]]
- [[Critical Mass]]
- [[Heat Sink]]

Fusion Augments:
- [[Battle Hardened]]
- [[Charitable]]
- [[Orbital Ward]]
- [[Thermal Recovery]]
- [[Tension]]
- [[Crescendo]]

Atom Augments:
- [[Steady Stride]]
- [[Quick Hands]]
- [[Last Legs]]
- [[Refresher]]
